# **Work Distribution**
We worked together by scheduling regular FaceTime calls, which allowed us to collaborate efficiently from different locations and make joint decisions on key elements such as the perspectives, the datastes, and the visualizations. Our task division was based on individual strengths, enabling each team member to contribute where they were most skilled. This approach not only improved our efficiency, but also strengthened the quality of the final product. The process felt like a truly collaborative effort.
